Before you click anything

The one idea: a traffic controller for sound

Sound comes IN on the left. You press a button to say where it goes OUT. That is the whole program.

A

A = Actual ears

Sound for YOU.
Goes to your real headphones or speakers.

B

B = Broadcast

Sound for OTHER PEOPLE.
Discord, Zoom and OBS hear this.

Potato, simplified - only 3 parts matter on Day 1
MICROPHONE
Input 1
Your voice
COMPUTER SOUND
VAIO strip
Videos, games, music
OUTPUTS
A1 + B1
A1 = ears
B1 = other people

Potato has 5 real inputs, 5 real outputs (A1-A5) and 3 pretend - virtual - ones (B1-B3). Ignore the rest for now. Beginners only need Input 1, A1 and B1.

CHECK: You can say it out loud - "A is for my ears. B is for other people." Then turn the page.

VBAN: sound over your network

VBAN sends sound to - and gets sound from - other computers and phones on the same home or office network. Click VBAN to open its settings box.

PartWhat it does
VBAN is ON / OFFTurns the whole VBAN service on or off.
UDP PortThe network "door" VBAN uses: 6980 by default. Two apps on one PC cannot share a port.
Incoming StreamsSound you want to hear from another computer. Name + IP address must match the sender. Right-click the name or address to pick a stream that was found. Switch it On.
Outgoing StreamsSound you send: pick the source bus (A or B), a stream name and the other computer's IP address. Switch it On.
Net QualityFAST for a good network; slower settings for a busy one.
Error lightsBlink red on problems: too many / broken / out-of-order / missing / too few packets.

Potato can hear 8 streams and send 8. Each: 11025 Hz to 96 kHz, 16 or 24 bit, 1 to 8 channels. Sending to an address ending in .255 reaches every computer on a wired network.

STOP - not needed for a normal setup. Leave VBAN alone unless your trainer sets it up. If Windows asks about the firewall for VBAN, ask your trainer.

INTELLIPAN: shape your voice

The dark square at the top of every hardware strip. It has 3 panels; right-click the panel to switch between them.

PanelWhat it does
Color Panel (first)Drag the dot to change the "colour" of the voice: 3 sound bands plus a tiny reverb in the top half. Up = more treble. Left = more bass, right = more medium. Top left = "double voice".
Position 3DA "binaural" effect: places the voice in a small virtual room, left or right. Stereo only. Helps when several people talk at once.
ModulationChorus-type effects. Bottom = chorus / phasing, top = modulation. Left side adds feedback. Further from the middle = stronger.
The 3 small dots under INTELLIPANEach turns orange when its panel is not neutral: 1st = Color, 2nd = Modulation, 3rd = 3D.
Try it now: Talk while you drag the Color Panel dot up - your voice gets brighter. Double-click the dot: it jumps back to neutral. (Double-click resets any control.)
STOP - for calls and training keep the dot neutral. All 3 dots dark = nothing changed.

When something goes wrong

No sound? Check in THIS order

Do not jump around. Start at 1. Most problems die at 1, 2 or 3.

1   Is Potato actually open?
No Potato open = no sound at all. Open it from the Start menu and leave it open.
2   Is A1 picked, top-right?
A1 must show your headphones or speakers (Step 3).
3   Is A1 lit up on the strip that is playing?
Dark A1 button = that sound goes nowhere you can hear (Step 6).
4   Still stuck? Restart the engine, not the computer
In Potato, click this real Menu button in the top-right corner:Then choose Restart Audio Engine. Then restart the app with no sound (Discord, OBS) - not the whole PC.
Hearing your own voice in your headphones? That is mic A1 switched ON by mistake. Turn mic A1 OFF, keep mic B1 ON (Step 6).
