Before you click anything

The one idea: a traffic controller for sound

Sound comes IN on the left. You press a button to say where it goes OUT. That is the whole program.

A

A = Actual ears

Sound for YOU.
Goes to your real headphones or speakers.

B

B = Broadcast

Sound for OTHER PEOPLE.
Discord, Zoom and OBS hear this.

Potato, simplified - only 3 parts matter on Day 1
MICROPHONE
Input 1
Your voice
COMPUTER SOUND
VAIO strip
Videos, games, music
OUTPUTS
A1 + B1
A1 = ears
B1 = other people

Potato has 5 real inputs, 5 real outputs (A1-A5) and 3 pretend - virtual - ones (B1-B3). Ignore the rest for now. Beginners only need Input 1, A1 and B1.

CHECK: You can say it out loud - "A is for my ears. B is for other people." Then turn the page.

The golden routing - set these buttons

Back in Potato. Every strip has small A and B buttons on its right side. Lit = ON. Dark = OFF. Set just these:

The only 2 strips you touch today
YOUR MICROPHONE
Input 1
A1 OFF

B1 ON
COMPUTER SOUND
VAIO strip
A1 ON

B1 OFF*

* Turn computer B1 ON only when people on a call should also hear your videos or game.

Why mic A1 is OFF: A1 is your own ears. Mic to A1 means hearing your own voice back in your headphones. Nobody wants that. Mic to B1 sends your voice to other people instead.

CHECK: Play a video - you hear it. Talk - the mic bars dance, but your voice is NOT in your headphones. Perfect. You are set up.

Way 3: Set it inside the app itself

Use this for calls and recording. Leave Windows alone. Open the settings inside Discord, Zoom or OBS and set these two lines:

Inside Discord / Zoom / OBS - Audio settings
1  Microphone / Input device

Voicemeeter Output (VB-Audio Voicemeeter VAIO)
This is B1 - your voice, already mixed by Potato
2  Speaker / Output device

Voicemeeter Aux Input (VB-Audio Voicemeeter AUX VAIO)
Call sound lands on its own AUX strip, separate from your game

This keeps call sound on the AUX strip, so you can turn the call up or down without touching game or music volume.

CHECK: Join a test call. The other person hears you, you hear them, and their voice moves the AUX strip - not the VAIO strip.

When something goes wrong

No sound? Check in THIS order

Do not jump around. Start at 1. Most problems die at 1, 2 or 3.

1   Is Potato actually open?
No Potato open = no sound at all. Open it from the Start menu and leave it open.
2   Is A1 picked, top-right?
A1 must show your headphones or speakers (Step 3).
3   Is A1 lit up on the strip that is playing?
Dark A1 button = that sound goes nowhere you can hear (Step 6).
4   Still stuck? Restart the engine, not the computer
In Potato: Menu > Restart Audio Engine. Then restart the app with no sound (Discord, OBS) - not the whole PC.
Hearing your own voice in your headphones? That is mic A1 switched ON by mistake. Turn mic A1 OFF, keep mic B1 ON (Step 6).
CHECK: Sound is back. Write down which number fixed it - that is your number next time too.
